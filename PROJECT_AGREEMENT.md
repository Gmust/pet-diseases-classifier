# Smart Pet Care App — Project Agreement

**Status:** proposed. This Agreement applies to an individual Contributor only after that
Contributor has explicitly accepted it (see [Acceptance](#12-acceptance)).

**Canonical location:** this file, in `Gmust/smart-pet-care-app`, is the canonical version of
the Agreement. Copies stored elsewhere are informational only.

---

## 1. Purpose

Smart Pet Care App is a collaborative student diploma project. This Agreement records, in plain
terms, how authorship is respected and how the participating Contributors may continue to use
the materials of the project.

It has two goals:

1. Each Contributor keeps the copyright in the original material they personally created.
2. Contributors who accept this Agreement grant the other participating Contributors and the
   Project a licence that allows the Project to be maintained, demonstrated and further
   developed as a whole.

This Agreement does not transfer copyright and does not attempt to measure or rank anyone's
contribution.

---

## 2. Definitions

**Project** — the Smart Pet Care App project as a whole, including the mobile client, the
backend API, the AI/ML service, designs, documentation and supporting project materials,
across all repositories, services and accounts where they are kept.

**Contributor** — a natural person who has contributed Project Materials to the Project.

**Participating Contributors** — Contributors who have accepted this Agreement and are
recorded in the Acceptance table.

**Project Materials** — original materials created specifically for the Project, including
without limitation:

- source code of the mobile/frontend client;
- source code of the backend and API;
- AI/ML and classifier code, together with models, weights, prompts, training and evaluation
  configuration created specifically for the Project, where applicable and where the
  Contributor is entitled to license them;
- APIs, schemas and API specifications;
- database schemas and migrations;
- tests and test fixtures;
- deployment scripts, infrastructure and configuration materials;
- CI/CD configuration;
- documentation of any kind, including diploma and project documentation;
- Figma designs, prototypes, wireframes and original design assets;
- Jira project documentation, requirements, specifications and ticket content;
- diagrams;
- presentations;
- technical specifications;
- any other copyrightable material created specifically for the Smart Pet Care App.

A material is not excluded from the Project Materials merely because it is stored in a
different repository, workspace, service or account, or because it has not yet been listed in
[`PROJECT_COMPONENTS.md`](PROJECT_COMPONENTS.md).

Third-party components (libraries, frameworks, datasets, pre-trained models, fonts, icons and
similar) are not Project Materials. They remain governed by their own licences, and nothing in
this Agreement grants rights in them.

## 3. Collaborative nature of the Project

Smart Pet Care App is a collaborative student project developed through the contributions of
multiple participants.

None of the following, by itself, establishes exclusive ownership of the Project as a whole:

- originating or proposing the initial idea or concept;
- creating the first repository;
- making the first commit;
- being the owner of a GitHub repository;
- being an administrator of a repository or GitHub organization;
- owning or administering the Jira workspace or project;
- owning or administering the Figma workspace or files;
- controlling hosting, cloud, deployment or database accounts.

This does not diminish individual authorship: each Contributor remains the author of the
specific material they created.

Each Contributor retains copyright in the original copyrightable Project Materials that
Contributor created, unless otherwise separately agreed in writing.

This Agreement does not make all Contributors joint owners of everything, and it does not
transfer any Contributor's copyright to any other Contributor or to the Project.

The resulting Smart Pet Care App is a combined work assembled from these individually authored
contributions, and its use by the Contributors is governed by the licence in Section 5.

## 6. Contributors leaving the team

If a Contributor leaves the diploma team, stops participating, becomes inactive, or loses
access to a repository, workspace, account or service, the licence that Contributor previously
granted under Section 5 is **not revoked** and remains in force.

The departure of a Contributor must not prevent the remaining Contributors from continuing,
maintaining, demonstrating, deploying, distributing or further developing Smart Pet Care App
using previously licensed Project Materials.

Accordingly, a Contributor who has accepted this Agreement may not, after leaving, require the
other Contributors to:

- remove previously contributed source code;
- remove backend or API functionality;
- remove AI or classifier functionality;
- remove previously provided designs or design assets;
- remove previously provided documentation;
- stop developing the application;
- stop using Project Materials already licensed under this Agreement.

At the same time, the remaining Contributors may not claim personal authorship of materials
created by another Contributor. Attribution and authorship records are preserved as described
in Section 8, and a departing Contributor keeps the copyright in their own contributions.

A Contributor may of course stop contributing new material at any time; this Agreement does
not oblige anyone to keep working on the Project.

## 7. Initial idea and concept

A Contributor may be recognized as the originator of the initial concept or idea for Smart Pet
Care App, and such recognition may be recorded in [`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](CONTRIBUTORS.md).

Such recognition acknowledges an individual contribution. It does not transfer ownership of
other Contributors' copyrightable work to that person, and it does not create exclusive
ownership of the resulting collaborative Project.

The further development, implementation, architecture, source code, design and documentation
remain subject to the rights of their respective authors and to this Agreement.

## 8. Attribution

Authorship and attribution records are preserved. Contributors do not remove or falsify
existing attribution in Git history, documentation, design files or project records, and do not
present another Contributor's work as their own.

Where the Project is presented, published or distributed, it is reasonable to describe it as a
collaborative project of its Contributors.

---

## 9. Repository, workspace and account ownership

Being the owner or administrator of:

- a GitHub repository;
- a GitHub organization;
- a Jira project or Atlassian workspace;
- a Figma workspace or file;
- a hosting account;
- a cloud account;
- a database or other service account;

does not by itself establish copyright ownership of materials created by other Contributors,
and does not establish exclusive ownership of Smart Pet Care App.

This is stated explicitly because the repositories and services of the Project are held under
the personal accounts of different Contributors. Administrative control over an account is a
practical arrangement, not a statement of authorship.

---

## 10. Project records

To identify authorship of specific materials, the Contributors may rely on objective project
records, including:

- Git commit history;
- pull requests;
- pull request reviews;
- GitHub activity;
- Jira history;
- Jira ticket authorship;
- documentation revision history;
- Figma version history;
- other objective project records.

These records are used to identify who authored particular materials. They are **not** used to
assign ownership percentages, to rank Contributors, or to determine automatically who
"contributed more".

Records may be incomplete or inaccurate (for example, shared accounts, pair work, mixed
aliases, or work done outside version control). Contributors may correct such records by
agreement, and corrections to alias mapping and contributed areas belong in
[`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](CONTRIBUTORS.md).

## 12. Acceptance

This Agreement applies to a Contributor only after that Contributor has explicitly accepted it.

**Merely adding this Agreement to an existing repository does not retroactively cause previous
Contributors to accept it.** Contributing to the Project before the Agreement existed is not
acceptance of it, and neither is continued participation on its own.

Acceptance may be documented by:

- a handwritten or electronic signature on this Agreement;
- an explicit written statement of acceptance (for example by email or a signed message)
  identifying this Agreement;
- explicit approval of a dedicated GitHub pull request created specifically for the adoption of
  this `PROJECT_AGREEMENT.md`, if the Contributors intend to use pull request approval as
  documented acceptance.

Approving a pull request is a record of what a person did and said; whether it also constitutes
a legally binding electronic signature depends on the applicable jurisdiction, and this
document makes no claim that it does everywhere. Contributors who want a stronger record may
sign the Agreement.
